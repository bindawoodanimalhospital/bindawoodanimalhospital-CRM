Getting started

One web address, any device, no installation.

Opening the CRM

The CRM is a website. Open it in Chrome, Edge or Safari on any computer, tablet or phone:

bdah-crm.bindawoodanimalhospital.workers.dev

Make it one tap awayOn the reception computer, bookmark the address. On a phone, open it and choose Add to Home screen; it then opens like an app. Everything works on a phone, including the queue, the pet store and the ward chart.

Signing in and out

  1. Type the email and password the owner gave you, then press Sign in.
  2. You land on Home, which shows only what your role is allowed to use.
  3. When you finish, open the menu at the bottom-left (your name) → Sign out — always on shared computers. Sign-ins and sign-outs are recorded.
Forgot your password?There is no self-service reset on purpose. Ask the owner, who resets it in Staff. If you see "your account isn't active yet", the owner hasn't activated your account — ask them to Activate it in Staff.

Accounts and roles

Everyone has their own login — never share it, because every action is recorded under the person signed in. What you see depends on your role (a person can have several):

The big picture — full flows

Four flows run the whole hospital. Learn these and every screen makes sense.

Everything hangs off two records: the pet owner (the paying client) and the pet (the patient). A family can share pets; each pet has one primary owner who receives reminders and bills.

Flow 1 — The patient's journey

Owner + pet registered→Check-in (token)→Waiting→With doctor→Consultation · Rx · vaccine · deworming · tests→Ready to pay→Bill & payment→Done

Side branches from the visit: Plan surgery → surgery flow (chapter 7) and Admit to ward → ward flow (chapter 8). Their work and costs stay linked to the same pet and visit.

Flow 2 — Money

Visit / pet store cart→Bill (draft)→Checkout→Paid in full
Checkout→Part / no payment → Pay later→Unpaid bill with promised date→Reminders→Receive payment→Closed

Corrections never delete money: a bill is voided, items are returned, money is refunded, a lost debt is written off — each with a reason, each recorded.

Flow 3 — Stock

Supplier delivery / Add stock / borrowed→Shelf (batches & expiry)→Billed medicine / pet-store sale / lent

Returns and cancelled orders put stock back. Shelf count corrects the numbers to what is really there. Low and expiring items appear on Home and in Inventory.

Flow 4 — Reminders

Doctor sets next date→Due & follow-ups→WhatsApp prepared (−3, 0, +3, +7 days)→Owner sends→Pet returns, dose recorded→Reminder closes itself

The same engine prepares follow-up reminders and payment reminders. If nothing happens, reception is alerted, then the owner (escalation).

Walkthrough: new client, sick pet

Mr. Bilal walks in with his dog Moti, who has been vomiting for two days. He has never been here before.

Register→Check in→Doctor→Prescription→Bill→Payment→Done

Reception 1 · Is he already registered?

  1. Open Today's queue → Check in a pet. Type the pet's or owner's name ("moti bilal").
  2. Nothing found? The dialog says "No pet found. Is this a new customer?" — press Register new owner + pet.

Reception 2 · Register owner and pet in one form

  1. Owner: full name (Urdu name optional), area, house, block, address, city, preferred contact and message language (Urdu/English), how they found us.
  2. If a similar name already exists the form warns "Possible existing customer" — open it instead of creating a duplicate.
  3. Pet (same form): name, species, breed, sex, neutered, date of birth or approximate age, colour, microchip.
  4. Save. Owner gets an ID like C-00012, the pet P-00034.
Phone numberReception doesn't type the phone number — it is private. Tell the owner (Dr. Musab) the client's number, or the owner adds it from the client's page → Edit. Reminders are sent by the owner using it.

Reception 3 · Check in

  1. Choose Moti, write the reason ("vomiting 2 days, not eating"), choose a doctor or Any doctor, tick Urgent if it is an emergency.
  2. Moti gets today's token (e.g. #7) and appears in Waiting on every screen at once. Tell Mr. Bilal his token.

Doctor 4 · Examine and treat

  1. On the queue press Call in on Moti's card (it moves to With doctor), then Open visit. Red alerts (allergies) show at the top.
  2. Consultation tab: complaint, history, vitals (temperature, heart rate, breathing, weight, mucous membranes, CRT, hydration, body condition, pain), examination, diagnosis (mark one primary), treatment and medicines given in the clinic. It saves as you type.
  3. Tests needed? Tests tab → order blood work / X-ray; press Tests on the queue card to move it to In treatment while waiting, and Treatment done when finished. Enter the results later.
  4. Prescriptions tab: each medicine with strength, dose, frequency, days, route, quantity and instructions → Issue prescription → print it for the owner.
  5. Follow-up needed? Set the follow-up date in the consultation — a reminder is created automatically.
  6. Press Finalize on the consultation, then Done with doctor on the queue card — it moves to Ready to pay.

Reception 5 · Bill and take payment

  1. On the visit press Bill this visit. Consultation, medicines given and items from the visit are offered in one tap; add anything else from the price list.
  2. Discount? Per line or for the whole bill, with a reason (large discounts need a manager's approval).
  3. Checkout: cash, card, bank transfer, JazzCash/Easypaisa (with transaction ID) — or split between methods. Paying only part? Choose Pay later (chapter 10).
  4. Print the receipt or the full bill, then Paid — finish on the queue card (Done). Billed medicines leave stock automatically.
What the system did for youCreated the records, the token and the visit timeline; linked the prescription and bill to Moti; reduced stock; created any follow-up reminder; and recorded who did each step.

Walkthrough: vaccination & deworming visit

Mrs. Sana brings her 10-week-old kitten Snowy for her first vaccine and deworming. Check in and bill as in chapter 4.

Doctor Record the vaccine

  1. Open the visit → Vaccines & deworming tab.
  2. Choose the vaccine (e.g. Biofel PCH), batch number, dose, route and site. If an approved schedule applies, the next dose name and date are suggested — check or change them.
  3. A blue What happens next box shows the exact dates: when the owner's WhatsApp reminder is prepared and when reception is alerted.
  4. Save. Any earlier reminder for this dose is closed automatically. Print the Vaccination card for Mrs. Sana from the same panel.

Doctor Record the deworming

  1. In the Deworming card below: dewormer given (suggestions from the price list), dose, weight.
  2. Next deworming due is suggested from age: every 2 weeks under 12 weeks, monthly until 6 months, then every 3 months. Tap 2 weeks / 1 month / 3 months or pick a date. Clear it for no reminder.
  3. Press Record deworming. Wrong entry (vaccine or deworming)? Void / Entered by mistake with a reason — the old reminder comes back.

Walkthrough: booked appointment & home visit

A client phones to book a grooming at home for Saturday.

Book→Confirmed→Check in→Visit→Bill (home visit charge)→Done
  1. Reception Appointments → Book appointment. Search the owner or pet name, choose the pet, type (e.g. Grooming), date, time and doctor.
  2. Where: At hospital or Home visit. For a home visit the address is filled from the client's profile — change it if the visit is elsewhere.
  3. On the day, open the appointment → Check in. It goes straight into today's queue with a token — no re-typing. If the pet is already in the queue, it joins that visit (no second token or bill).
  4. Doctor works on the visit as usual. The visit shows Home visit with the address.
  5. Reception Bill this visit — the bill inherits "Home visit" and its address; add the home visit charge (price editable by distance).

When things change

  • Client cancels — Cancel the appointment with a reason.
  • Client doesn't come — on the appointment's ⋮ menu choose Didn't come (no-show); it shows in their history and the Alert centre to rebook.
  • Wrong place — on the visit or bill press Change next to the service location.

Walkthrough: a surgery case

Bruno needs a spay. Consent and pre-op checks are required before the operation can start.

Plan→Scheduled→Consent→Admitted→Pre-op check→Surgery→Recovery→Going home
  1. Doctor From the visit press Plan surgery (or Surgery → Plan a surgery): procedure, why (indication), urgency, surgeon, date & time, estimate told to owner (Rs.) with notes, and instructions before surgery (e.g. fasting).
  2. Mark as scheduled when the date is fixed. It appears on the Surgery board.
  3. Reception / Doctor Owner consent: print the consent form (Urdu or English) for the owner to sign → Record consent: who gave consent, relationship, how (signed paper / signed on screen / verbal by phone), confirm risks and estimate were explained.
  4. On the day: Pet arrived — admit.
  5. Start pre-op: weight, temperature, heart and breathing rate, ASA class, fasting confirmed, the checklist → Mark pre-op check complete.
  6. Start surgery — the system refuses without consent and the pre-op check. (A life-saving emergency uses Start emergency surgery with a reason, recorded.)
  7. Anaesthesia & operation: drugs, times, team, operation notes, complications, Materials used.
  8. Operation finished — recovery: recovery notes. Need more care? Keep in ward (chapter 8). Complication? Back to theatre.
  9. Discharge — going home: home-care instructions (printed), post-op check date → print the discharge sheet.
Cancelling and reopeningCancel surgery asks for a reason (e.g. owner withdrew consent). A discharged record can be reopened by a senior doctor with a reason; the discharged version is kept.

Walkthrough: a ward admission

Coco has parvo and needs IV fluids for three days.

Admit→Treatment chart→Doses given / not given→Notes & vitals→Discharge
  1. Doctor From the visit press Admit to ward (or Ward → Admit a pet): reason for stay ("Parvo — IV fluids & monitoring"), kennel / cage, doctor in charge, expected to go home, feeding plan ("nothing by mouth 12 h"), care / handling notes ("bites when touched").
  2. Treatment chart → add each treatment: what (Ringer's lactate, Ceftriaxone…), dose, route (IV/SC/oral), every (hours), first dose at, for how many days, instructions.
  3. Ward staff The ward board shows every dose that is due. When given, press Given; if not, Not given with the reason. Time and person are recorded.
  4. Add Notes & vitals during the stay. Changed plan? Stop a treatment (with a note) and add the new one.
  5. Discharge: outcome, summary of the stay, home-care instructions, check-up date → print the discharge sheet. Bill the stay from the visit.
Night shiftOpen Ward on a phone — the due doses are listed in time order, so nothing is missed between shifts.

Walkthrough: the pet store

A walk-in buyer, a phone order for home delivery, and a cancelled order.

Store staff A · Walk-in customer buys cat food — in seconds

Name + Enter→Tap / scan→Cash, Enter→Next customer
  1. Open Pet store. Ask the customer's name and type it in the Customer name box. A registered client shows up — tap them. Otherwise press Enter (or tap Bill as walk-in: Ali): the name goes on the bill and receipt, with no registration and no phone number.
  2. The quick picks at the top are the 8 best sellers of the last 30 days — tap one to add it.
  3. Or scan the barcode / type in the search; when only one product matches, Enter adds it. Products with sizes show one card with a button per size; greyed-out sizes are not available.
  4. Change quantity in the cart if needed. Items with a typed price show a price box.
  5. Type the cash received and press Enter. The sale finishes on the same screen showing change due and a print button — the screen is ready for the next customer.
Name optional, registration not neededA walk-in sale works with no name at all, or with just a walk-in name (shown as "Ali (walk-in)" in Billing and on the receipt). A registered client is needed only for home delivery, pay later, or to keep the sale on their account.

Store staff B · Phone order for home delivery, cash on delivery

Cart + owner→Home delivery→On delivery→Dispatch (rider)→Delivered→Receive payment
  1. Add the products, then attach the pet owner (search name or pet).
  2. Choose Home delivery. The address fills from their profile — correct it for this order if needed. Check the delivery charge (Rs. 50 by default from the price list; change by distance — only staff allowed to give discounts can make it Rs. 0).
  3. How will they pay? Pay now (cash, card, bank, JazzCash/Easypaisa — enter the transaction ID), On delivery (cash on delivery), or Pay later (optional date).
  4. Confirm and print the receipt to send with the order.
  5. Open Home deliveries: the order is Pending. Before it leaves you can Edit the address or charge. Press Dispatch and write the rider's name.
  6. When it arrives: Delivered. For cash on delivery, record the money the rider brings with Receive payment on the bill. If it stays unpaid after delivery, payment reminders start.

Store staff C · The customer cancels

  1. On the order (in Home deliveries or on the bill) press Cancel order. Type a reason if you like ("Customer cancelled on the phone") — it's optional.
  2. The system refunds any money taken (same method), puts the stock back on the shelf, closes any unpaid balance, marks the bill "Cancelled", and records it in the activity log.
  3. Find it later under Cancelled in Home deliveries.
Who can cancelStore staff: orders not yet delivered and today's counter sales. Delivered or older orders: the owner. A product can never be sold at Rs. 0 — the system refuses.

Walkthrough: money owed & collecting it

Mr. Kamran's bill is Rs. 8,000. He pays Rs. 3,000 now and promises the rest next week.

Checkout: part payment→Pay later→Unpaid bills→Reminder→Receive payment→Closed
  1. Reception At checkout enter Rs. 3,000 and choose Pay later. Note and Expected by date are optional — left empty it says "Payment to follow", a week from today.
  2. The bill is issued; Rs. 5,000 appears in Unpaid bills and on his page as Owes Rs. 5,000 (also in search results). Larger amounts may show "waiting for manager approval" — a manager presses Approve in Unpaid bills or the Alert centre.
  3. Around the promised date a payment reminder is prepared in Messages to send. Overdue amounts are highlighted and appear in Needs attention.
  4. He pays: open the bill (or his page → Account history) → Receive payment → amount and method. The bill closes when the balance is zero.

Walkthrough: stock arrives, counts & corrections

From the supplier's van to an accurate shelf.

Inventory A · A supplier delivery with a bill

Suppliers→Receive a delivery→Lines: qty · cost · batch · expiry→Stock up→Pay supplier
  1. Suppliers → choose the supplier (or New supplier once: name, contact person, phone, address, NTN) → Receive a delivery.
  2. Enter the supplier's bill number and date, then Add line for each product: quantity in pieces, cost per piece, batch number and expiry.
  3. Check the total against the paper bill → Receive into stock. Stock goes up, cost is kept for profit reports, and You owe increases.
  4. When you pay them: Pay supplier → amount, method, paid on, reference / cheque no. Deliveries and Payments made show the history.

Inventory B · Quick top-up without a supplier bill

  1. Open the product (Inventory → tap it) → Add stock.
  2. Type how many pieces arrived. Batch and expiry are optional. Save — tracking switches on by itself.

Inventory C · Shelf count — make the numbers true

  1. Inventory → Shelf count. Every product is listed with what the system thinks is there.
  2. Count the shelf and type the real number in each box (leave untouched ones empty). Add a note such as "Monthly count".
  3. Save. Where the shelf has less, the soonest-expiring stock is taken off first; where it has more, the extra is added. Every change is recorded. The Uncounted tile on Inventory shows products never counted.
First timeDo one full shelf count when you start, then monthly. For a single product use Set the count on its page.

Inventory D · Sizes — one product, many weights

  1. New product sold in sizes: New item → Product with sizes → name once, then each size with its price (and opening quantity).
  2. Existing separate products ("RC Kitten 2kg", "RC Kitten 4kg")? Inventory → Sizes shows suggestions — combine them in one tap.
  3. A size runs out at the distributor? On the product's Sizes card switch it to not available; it greys out in the pet store and is flagged on bills. Switch back when it returns.

Inventory E · Borrowing from / lending to a nearby shop

  1. Borrowed & lent stock → record borrowed or lent, the partner, products, quantities and the expected return date.
  2. Stock moves immediately. When it comes back (all or part), record the return on the loan. Overdue loans show on Home.

Surgery & ward

Reference — the boards and the rules.

Surgery board

Surgery lists planned, scheduled and in-progress operations with consent and pre-op status. Each surgery page follows: Plan → Estimate → Owner consent → Pre-op check (on the day) → Anaesthesia & operation + Materials used → Recovery & going home → History. Full flow in chapter 7.

RuleWhy
No start without consent and pre-op checkProtects the pet, the owner and the doctor.
Emergency start needs a reasonLife-saving cases are possible but always explained.
Consent can't be editedIf the owner withdraws, revoke it (reason) and record a new one.
Signer's phone on consentEntered and seen by the hospital owner only.

Ward board

Ward shows each admitted pet with kennel, reason, doctor, expected discharge and doses due now/overdue. Each stay page has Why here, Plan, Treatment chart (Given / Not given / Stop), Notes & vitals, Owner and Discharge. Full flow in chapter 8.

Billing, payments & unpaid bills

Reference — accurate bills and every rupee accounted for.

Billing list

Billing shows clinic bills and pet store sales with status, today's collected total and Cash in drawer (today) (cash received − cash refunds). New bill starts a bill for any owner; most bills start from a visit with Bill this visit.

A bill's life

Draft→Checkout (issued)→Paid / part paid→Return · Refund · Void
  • Draft — add services/products (Add service or product), quantities, item discounts, bill discount (reason), tax. Price-list prices are fixed; items like "Other charge" let you type the price.
  • Checkout — one or several payment methods; pay later for the rest. A double tap or lost internet never records a payment twice.
  • Issued — locked. Print the bill or receipt. Receive payment for later payments.

Corrections — never delete, always explain

ActionWhenWhat happens
Return itemsProduct/medicine brought backChoose lines and quantities; refund calculated fairly even with discounts; stock goes back.
RefundMoney back without a returnWith reason and method (shown in Data checks).
Void billBill made by mistakeCancelled with a reason; stays visible.
Cancel orderPet-store / delivery orderRefund + stock back + void in one step (chapter 9).
Write offDebt will never be paidManager/owner, with reason.

Unpaid bills

Unpaid bills lists every balance with promised date, days late (colour-coded) and approval status. Actions: Receive payment, New promised date, Approve / Not approved (manager), Write off. Total outstanding is shown at the top.

Owner account & statement

Owner page → Account & statement: Account history (every bill and payment, charged vs paid/credit, balance), Receive payment towards the whole balance (oldest bills first, extra kept as advance), Take advance / deposit (e.g. surgery deposit), Statement print.

Follow-ups: due list, messages, tasks, alerts

Reference — the system remembers so staff don't have to.

Due & follow-ups

Vaccine doses, deworming and follow-up visits, under Upcoming, Today and Overdue. Each line: pet, owner, what is due, date, days late. Actions:

  • Contacted — record what the owner said (booked, will come…).
  • New date — reschedule with a note.
  • Not needed — close it with a reason (e.g. pet moved away).
  • Recording the dose on a visit closes it automatically. Tap How next-dose reminders work at the top for the full explanation.

Messages to send

Prepared every 30 min→Open WhatsApp→Press send in WhatsApp→Sent

Vaccine, deworming, follow-up and payment reminders, in the owner's language. Press Open WhatsApp (message already written) → send → Sent. Or Couldn't reach / Skip with a note. Calls show Called — done. Every message stays in the owner's history. Phone numbers are private, so only the hospital owner sends them; others see "the number is private — the owner sends this one".

Campaigns

Messages → Campaigns: friendly messages to a group — pets with a birthday this month or pets not seen for 6+ months. Name it, write the message; it goes into Messages to send in each owner's language. Only owners who opted in to campaign messages are included.

Tasks

New task: what, for whom, due time, priority (Low / Normal / High), linked owner/pet. Flow: open → Start → done (write what was done) or Cancel task (why). Comments with Add. Your tasks show on Home; overdue ones in red.

Alert centre

Everything unfinished across the hospital, each with what to do: unfinished records (finish & finalize), tests pending / to review, overdue vaccines and follow-ups, missed appointments (call to rebook), pay-later approvals (approve or reject), unpaid and promised-today bills, low/expiring stock, escalated items. Viewing doesn't close an item — finish it in its own screen. On an overdue item or unpaid bill, Pause reminders stops its reminders until a date, with a required reason (e.g. owner abroad until the 15th).

Privacy & built-in safety

Protections the system enforces on its own — in the database, not just on screen.

Pet owners' phone numbers

Phone numbers, WhatsApp numbers and email are visible only to the hospital owner: not on screens, prints, search, messages or the activity log for anyone else, and other staff can't search by number. The permission can't be given to another role. Addresses are visible to staff so deliveries and home visits work.
