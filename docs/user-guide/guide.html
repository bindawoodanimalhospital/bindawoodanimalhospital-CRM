Getting started

One web address, any device, no installation.

Opening the CRM

The CRM is a website. Open it in Chrome, Edge or Safari on any computer, tablet or phone:

bdah-crm.bindawoodanimalhospital.workers.dev

Tip — make it one tap awayOn the reception computer, bookmark the address. On a phone, open it and choose Add to Home screen; it then opens like an app.

Signing in

  1. Type the email and password the hospital admin gave you, then press Sign in.
  2. You land on the Home page, which shows only what your role is allowed to use.
  3. When you finish, open the menu at the bottom-left (your name) and choose Sign out — especially on shared computers.
Forgot your password?There is no self-service reset on purpose. Ask the Owner/Admin, who can reset it from Staff. Every reset is recorded in the activity log.

Accounts and roles

Each staff member has their own login. Never share logins — the system records who did what, and it can only be accurate if everyone uses their own account. What each person sees and can do depends on their role:

How everything connects

Understand this page and the rest of the system makes sense.

Everything in the CRM hangs off two records: the pet owner (the paying customer) and the pet (the patient). Each visit of a pet produces the medical work, and the medical work produces the bill.

Pet owner→Pet→Visit (token)→Consultation→Prescription · Vaccine · Test→Bill→Payment
Owner ↔ Pets

One owner, many pets — one pet, several owners

A family can share a pet. Each pet has one primary owner who receives reminders and bills.

Visit

Every time a pet comes in

Checking a pet in creates a visit with today's token number. Everything the doctor does is saved inside that visit.

Bill

Built from the visit

Services, medicines and vaccines from the visit go onto one bill. Medicines on a bill reduce stock automatically.

Unpaid bill

Pay later, with a promise date

If an owner pays part — or nothing yet — the rest becomes an unpaid bill. The reason and promised date are optional; payment is recorded whenever it arrives.

Reminders

Created automatically

A vaccine or deworming sets the next due date; a consultation can set a follow-up. These appear in Due & follow-ups and become WhatsApp messages.

From a visit

Surgery and ward stays

A doctor can plan surgery or admit the pet to the ward from the visit; their costs join the pet's bills.

Stock

Pet store and medicines

Pet-store sales and billed medicines take stock out; supplier deliveries and borrowed stock put it back in.

Everything

Recorded in the activity log

Every sign-in, page opened, print and change — who, what and when — is kept and cannot be edited.

In one sentenceRegister the owner and pet once; after that every visit, medicine, vaccine, bill, payment, call and reminder is linked to them, so the full history is always one tap away.

Pet owners & pets

Register once, use forever.

Register a new owner and their pet

  1. Press + New → New pet owner + pet (or use the quick action on Home).
  2. Enter the owner's name, area and address (house, block, street, city) and preferred contact. Phone, WhatsApp and email are optional and only the owner of the hospital can add or see them — other staff see a lock note instead.
  3. Add the pet on the same form: name, species, breed, sex, date of birth (or estimated age), colour, microchip.
  4. Save. The owner gets an ID like C-00012 and the pet P-00034.
Avoid duplicatesBefore registering, search the owner's name and their pet's name. The form warns you when a similar name already exists (and, for the owner, when the number is already saved). If two records of the same person were created, the owner can Merge them from the owner page — visits, bills, messages and tasks all move to the kept record.

The owner page

Shows the address (phone numbers and email for the owner only), all their pets, money owed, home deliveries and home visits, and the contact history (calls, WhatsApps). Buttons at the top let you Call, Edit, start a New bill, a Pet store sale, Book appointment, open Full history — all pets and the Account & statement.

The pet page

  • Alerts — allergies, conditions and handling warnings. Critical ones show in red at the top of the pet and in every search result. Add them as soon as you learn them.
  • Weight — record weights; the change since last time is shown.
  • Owners — add a second owner, or make someone the primary owner.
  • Medical history and Due — a summary; press Full medical history for everything (chapter 12).
Phone numbers are privateOnly the hospital owner can see, add or change pet owners' phone numbers, WhatsApp and email — on screens, prints, search and messages. This can't be given to any other role. Addresses are visible to staff so deliveries and home visits work.

Today's queue & appointments

From arrival to the doctor.

Check a pet in

  1. Open Today's queue and press Check in a pet.
  2. Search the owner or pet, choose the pet, write the reason ("vomiting since 2 days"), choose the doctor if requested, and mark Urgent for emergencies.
  3. The pet receives today's token number. Tell the owner their token.

The queue board updates live on every screen. Each card moves through these stages:

Waiting→With doctor→In treatment→Ready to bill→Completed

The doctor presses Call in to take the next patient. Urgent cases are highlighted and waiting time is shown on every card. Tokens restart at 1 every day (Pakistan time).

One pet, one visit per dayIf a pet that is already in the queue arrives for an appointment, it is linked to the existing visit — no second token, no second bill.

Appointments

  1. Open Appointments and press Book for phone or WhatsApp bookings.
  2. Choose owner, pet, type, date and time, doctor, and whether it is at the clinic or a home visit (with address).
  3. When the owner arrives, press Check in on the appointment — it goes straight into today's queue.

Appointments can be confirmed, cancelled (with a reason) or marked did not come. Missed appointments show in the owner's history so reception can follow up.

6

The visit: doctor's work

Everything for one pet's visit lives on one screen.

Open the visit from the queue. The top shows the pet, owner, token and any allergy alert. Below are tabs: Consultation Prescriptions Vaccines & deworming Tests. Buttons on top: Bill this visit, Plan surgery, Admit to ward, Full history.

Consultation notes

  1. Record the complaint and history, then vitals: temperature, heart rate, breathing rate, weight, mucous membranes, CRT, hydration, body condition and pain score.
  2. Write the examination, assessment and diagnosis (mark the main one as primary), treatment given and medicines given in the clinic.
  3. Set a follow-up plan and date if needed — this creates a reminder automatically.
  4. Notes save automatically as you type. Press Finalize when complete.
Finalized notes are lockedOnce finalized, notes cannot be silently changed. To correct them a doctor must re-open with a reason; every version is kept. Doctor's private notes are never printed.

Prescriptions

Add each medicine with strength, dose, how often, for how long, route, quantity and instructions, plus advice for the owner. Check carefully, then Issue — it locks and is ready to print. To change an issued prescription, cancel it and issue a new one.

Vaccines

Record the vaccine, batch number, dose, route and site. The next due date is calculated from the vaccine schedule and appears in Due & follow-ups. If recorded by mistake, void it with a reason — the reminder it closed is re-opened automatically. A printable vaccination card is available from the pet.

When a next dose date is set, a blue What happens next box shows the exact days the owner's WhatsApp reminder is prepared and when reception is alerted.

Available vaccines include the Bioveta range: Biofel PCH and PCHR (cats), Biocan Puppy, Biocan Novel DHPPi/L4 and DHPPi/L4R, Biocan R (rabies) and Biocan M (ringworm). The owner or a senior doctor can add more in Vaccines & schedules.

Deworming

  1. On the Vaccines & deworming tab, fill in Dewormer given (suggestions come from the price list), dose and weight.
  2. Next deworming due is suggested from the pet's age — every 2 weeks under 12 weeks old, monthly until 6 months, then every 3 months. Use the 2 weeks / 1 month / 3 months buttons or pick any date; clear it for no reminder.
  3. Press Record deworming. The pet appears in Due & follow-ups on that date and reminders follow, like vaccines. A wrong entry is undone with Entered by mistake (with a reason).

Tests

Order blood work, X-ray, ultrasound and other tests with the reason and urgency. When results arrive, enter the findings, values and impression; a senior doctor reviews them. Upload reports and images to the pet's documents.

Surgery & ward

Planned and emergency procedures, and pets staying overnight.

Surgery

  1. From the visit press Plan surgery: procedure, reason, urgency, surgeon, date and estimate.
  2. Consent: print the consent form for the owner to sign, then record who signed and how (the signer's phone field is shown to the hospital owner only).
  3. Pre-op: weight, vitals, ASA class, fasting confirmed and the pre-op checklist.
  4. In theatre: anaesthesia, start and end times, team, notes, complications and materials used.
  5. Recovery and discharge: recovery notes, home-care instructions, follow-up date, and print the discharge sheet.

Surgery in the menu shows everything scheduled and in progress.

Ward (hospital stays)

  1. From the visit press Admit to ward: reason, kennel, attending doctor, feeding plan.
  2. Add treatment orders: medicine or fluid, dose, route and how often (every X hours).
  3. The ward board shows doses due. When given, staff press Given (or skipped/refused with a note) — the time and the person are recorded.
  4. Add notes and vitals during the stay. On discharge, write the summary and instructions, and print the discharge sheet.
8

Billing & payments

Accurate bills, part payments and promises, all tracked.

Bill a visit

  1. On the visit press Bill this visit (or from the owner page, New bill).
  2. Add services and products from the price list. Medicines and vaccines from the visit can be added in one tap. Some items (like "Other charge") let you type the price.
  3. Discounts: per line or for the whole bill, with a reason. Large discounts need a manager's approval.
  4. Press Checkout: choose the payment method(s) — cash, card, bank transfer, JazzCash/Easypaisa — and the amount. You can split between methods.
  5. If the owner pays only part or nothing yet, choose Pay later. The note and promised date are optional (if left empty: "Payment to follow", 7 days). The remainder becomes an unpaid bill; record the payment when it arrives.
  6. Print the bill or the receipt, or share it.
Double-tap protectionIf the checkout button is pressed twice or the internet drops mid-payment, the system records the payment only once.

Unpaid bills

Unpaid bills lists every balance with its promised date; overdue ones are highlighted. Record later payments from the bill. Payment reminders are prepared automatically for WhatsApp (chapter 11).

Returns, refunds and voids

  • Return items — choose the lines and quantities; the refund is calculated fairly even when the bill had a discount, and stock goes back.
  • Refund — money back without a return, with a reason (shown in Data checks).
  • Void — cancel a bill made by mistake, with a reason. Voided bills remain visible.

Owner account & statement

From the owner page, Account & statement shows every bill and payment over time and what is still owed. Print the statement for the owner.

Pet store & home delivery

Fast counter sales, deliveries and cash on delivery.

Quick sale for walk-in customers

  1. Open Pet store. The quick picks at the top are the 8 best sellers of the last 30 days — one tap adds one to the cart.
  2. Or scan the barcode / type in the search box; when only one product matches, Enter adds it.
  3. Type the cash received and press Enter to pay. The sale finishes on the same screen (with change due and a print button) — ready for the next customer.

Products with sizes

A product sold in several weights or sizes (e.g. 1 kg, 3 kg, 10 kg) shows as one card with a button per size. Sizes that are not available right now are greyed out and can't be added. Prices with an editable amount show a price box in the cart.

Home delivery and payment

  1. Attach the sale to a pet owner, choose Home delivery, check the address (filled from their profile) and the delivery charge.
  2. Choose how they pay: Pay now (cash, card, bank, JazzCash/Easypaisa with transaction ID), On delivery (cash on delivery), or Pay later (optional date).
  3. Print the receipt. Products are always billed at their price — a product can never go out at Rs. 0.

Home deliveries

Home deliveries lists orders to dispatch and on the way. Mark Dispatched (with rider) and then Delivered. For cash on delivery, payment reminders start only after the order is delivered and still unpaid.

Cancelling an order

Press Cancel order on the delivery or on the bill, optionally with a reason. Any money taken is refunded, the stock goes back on the shelf, and the cancellation is recorded in the activity log. Cancelled orders are listed under Cancelled on Home deliveries.

Who can cancelStore staff can cancel orders not yet delivered and today's counter sales. Older or delivered orders are cancelled by the owner.

Home visits

Appointments and visits can be marked as home visit with the address; a home visit charge can be added to the bill. They appear in the owner's service history.

Inventory & suppliers

Know what you have, what is expiring and what to order.

Stock and the price list

Inventory has two views: Stock (quantities, batches, expiry) and Price list (every service and product with its price). Summary tiles show low / reorder, expiring in 60 days and expired on the shelf. Press New item to add a product or service. For products you can type the opening quantity (how many pieces are on the shelf now), and choose Product with sizes to add all sizes at once.

Adding stock — one step

  1. Open the product and press Add stock.
  2. Type how many pieces arrived. Batch and expiry are optional. Save — stock tracking switches on by itself.

Shelf count

Inventory → Shelf count lists every product with a box for the number actually on the shelf. Type the counts and save; the system corrects the stock (taking away the soonest-expiring first) and records it. The Uncounted tile shows products never counted.

Sizes and availability

A product's page has a Sizes card: add a size, and switch each size available / not available. Inventory → Sizes suggests products that look like sizes of the same item (e.g. "Royal Canin 2kg" and "Royal Canin 4kg") — combine them in one tap.

Receive stock from a supplier

  1. Open Suppliers (add the supplier once), then Receive stock.
  2. Add each product with quantity (pieces), cost, batch number and expiry date.
  3. Save — stock increases and the cost is recorded for profit reports. Record supplier payments on the supplier page.

Borrowed & lent stock

When a nearby pet shop or clinic borrows from you, or you borrow from them, record it in Borrowed & lent stock with the expected return date. Stock moves immediately; record returns (full or part) when they come back. Overdue loans appear on the Home page.

Good habitCheck Expiring in 60 days every Monday and use or return those items first.
11

Follow-ups & reminders

The system remembers so staff don't have to.

Due & follow-ups

Every vaccine due, deworming due, booster and follow-up appears here with views for Today, Overdue and Upcoming. Reception calls or messages the owner and records the outcome (booked, will come, not reachable). Recording the vaccine closes the reminder automatically.

How next-dose reminders work (vaccines & deworming)

  1. The doctor sets the next date when recording the vaccine or deworming (a schedule or the pet's age suggests it). No date = no reminder.
  2. It appears in Due & follow-ups under Upcoming → Today → Overdue.
  3. WhatsApp messages are prepared automatically 3 days before, on the day, and 3 and 7 days after the due date, in the owner's language, in Messages to send.
  4. If the pet doesn't come, reception is alerted (on the day and 3 days after), and it is escalated to the owner if still overdue.
  5. It stops by itself the moment the next dose is recorded. Marking a record "entered by mistake" brings the old reminder back.

The same explanation is on the Due & follow-ups and Reminder settings pages. Days and wording can be changed in Reminder settings.

Messages to send

Every 30 minutes the system prepares reminders (vaccines due, follow-ups, payment reminders) according to Reminder settings. In Messages to send, press the WhatsApp button on each — WhatsApp opens with the message already written — send it, and mark it sent. Because phone numbers are private, only the hospital owner can send them; other staff see "the number is private — the owner sends this one". Calls and in-person conversations can also be logged on the owner page.

Tasks

Create tasks for yourself or a colleague ("Call Mrs. Khan about lab result"), with a due time and linked owner. Your open tasks show on Home; overdue ones are in red.

Alert centre & notifications

The bell shows notifications for you. Alert centre gathers clinical and stock alerts that need a decision.

Full histories

The complete story of a pet, or of a whole family.

A pet's full medical history

On any pet press Full medical history. Every visit is shown newest first, and opening one shows everything:

  • Who brought the pet, the doctor, who checked it in, and a timeline of the visit with times.
  • Consultation: complaint, vitals, examination, diagnosis, treatment, medicines given in the clinic, follow-up.
  • Each prescription with dose, frequency, duration and who prescribed; vaccines with batch and who gave them; tests with who ordered, entered and reviewed them.
  • Surgery with surgeon and team; ward stays with every dose given and by whom; and the bill.

The top shows a summary: visits, first and last seen, vaccinations, surgeries, hospital stays, total billed and still owed, every doctor who has seen the pet and every medicine ever prescribed. Filter by type, doctor or year, or search inside the history.

An owner's full history — all pets

On the owner page press Full history — all pets. One timeline for the whole family: every visit of every pet in full detail, plus appointments (including missed ones), payments and refunds with who received them, and every call or WhatsApp with its outcome.

Print for referrals or the ownerBoth histories have Print full history, producing a clean document on the clinic letterhead. Doctors' private notes are never printed.

Expenses & reports

Where the money goes, and how the clinic is doing.

Expenses

Record every expense (rent, electricity, salaries, supplies) with category, amount and payment method. Expenses feed into the profit estimate.

Reports

Pick a period (today, last 7 or 30 days, this month, last month, last 3 months, this year or custom dates) and a report. Every report can be exported.

ReportWhat it tells you
MoneyBilled, collected (cash actually received), owed to the clinic and estimated profit after stock cost and expenses; by payment method and by service.
Clinic activityVisits, new owners and pets, appointments kept and missed, busiest days, work per doctor.
Medicines & stockWhat was used and sold, stock value, low and expiring items.
Data checksThings that need tidying: bills owing with no promise date, refunds without a return, duplicate-looking owners and similar.
"Billed" vs "Collected"Billed is the value of bills issued; collected is cash actually received in the period. The difference is what owners still owe.

Owner & admin

Staff, permissions, oversight and clinic settings.

Staff

  1. Staff → Add staff: name, email, phone, title, whether they are a doctor, and their role(s).
  2. Give them their email and temporary password. Reset passwords from the same page.
  3. When someone leaves, turn Access off — they are signed out immediately everywhere, and their history stays.

Who can do what

A grid of roles and permissions. Tick or untick to change what a role can see and do. Changes apply immediately to everyone with that role, and are recorded. "See & edit phone numbers and email" is locked to the Owner and can't be given to any other role.

Activity log — every action, by every person

The owner sees everything staff do in the CRM, in plain sentences, grouped by day:

Sign-ins & sign-outs

With the device and IP address, and wrong-password attempts.

Every change

Added, changed, removed — down to a single field, with before → after values.

Pages opened & prints

What each person looked at and printed.

Calls, WhatsApp, searches

Numbers tapped to call or message, what was searched, text copied (amount, not content).

Away, back, tab closed

Idle after 10 minutes, the return, and closing the CRM.

Blocked pages

Attempts to open pages a person isn't allowed to.

The cards at the top show when each staff member last signed in and was last active (green dot = active in the last 15 minutes). Filter by person, kind of action, record type and dates. The log loads 60 entries at a time — scroll down (or press Load more) for older ones. Entries can never be edited or deleted.

Settings

  • Settings — clinic name, address, phone/WhatsApp (03331606689), email and details printed on bills, prescriptions and certificates; billing rules such as discount approval limits.
  • Vaccines & schedules — add or edit vaccines, and the schedules used to calculate next due dates. New schedules start as drafts and only suggest dates after a senior doctor approves them.
  • Reminder settings — which reminders are prepared, when, and the message wording.

Built-in safety

Protections the system enforces on its own.
